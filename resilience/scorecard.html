When Things Go Wrong — Resilience Scorecard

For each statement, rate yourself from:

  • 1 = Rarely true
  • 2 = Sometimes true
  • 3 = Often true
  • 4 = Usually true
  • 5 = Very true

Be honest. This is not a test of perfection, but a reflection of how you usually respond during difficult seasons. This scorecard is designed for reflection and self-awareness, not as a clinical psychological assessment.
